The matric season advantage

South Africa has ~600,000 matric students writing exams every year. Results drop in January — that's your traffic tsunami. If you launch by August and build up SEO content through October, you'll capture massive organic search traffic in November–January when every matric student is Googling "where can I apply with my marks". This is your window.
